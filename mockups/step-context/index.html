Keeping the lifecycle in view

Four candidate patterns so a learner inside a process step (e.g. Opportunity 1 · Step 1 — Self Assessment) always knows which stage of the IDENTIFY cycle they are in. Each screen below is the same Step 1 page with one device added, highlighted in gold. These are composable — B + D, or A + C, pair well.

A

Persistent mini-ring dock

A small, always-on lifecycle ring pinned to the bottom-left of the reading pane (mirroring the “Contents” / “Ask the Playbook” floating buttons). The active segment is gold with the step label beside it. Tapping it expands to the full diagram. It survives scrolling, so the anchor is never off-screen — this is the only option that is visible 100% of the time.

B

Pinned step bar under the header

A slim sticky stepper that appears on any chapter that belongs to a lifecycle: breadcrumb on the left (“Opportunity 1 · Identify”), then the four steps as a segmented bar — completed in sage, current in gold with an underline. It reads like a progress bar for the process itself and doubles as navigation between steps.

C

“You are here” context card

A context card at the top of every step page: mini-ring on the left, position (“Step 1 of 4 · Identify”), the step name in display type, and prev/next step links. No new chrome — it is part of the content, so it costs nothing on mobile and can carry a one-line “what this step is for”.
